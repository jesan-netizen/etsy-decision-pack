56 young winners, 26,429 listing rows, counted on a real 6-month sales window. The lane map, what each lane actually yields, and the shape decision that drives everything.

Source: /home/ubuntu/etsy_phase2/yw_listings.csv Analysis basis: de-duplicated to 25,704 unique listings across 56 shops (604 exact-duplicate rows removed; 725 further rows collapsed by identical Shop + Product Link after case/whitespace variation). What this corpus actually is: 66.9% of titles contain the phrase "digital download", 36.5% contain "printable", 58.2% contain "wall art" or "print"/"poster". It is a single-product vertical — digital wall art — with eleven aesthetic sub-lanes. There is no meaningful clipart lane (2 titles mention clipart), no planner lane (4), no sticker lane (1), no SVG lane (1). The nearest adjacent formats are cross stitch (46), sewing patterns (30) and woodworking (22), all under 0.2% of listings. Any plan for this corpus should be read as a digital-print playbook, not a general Etsy playbook.

There is no decay in this corpus — there is only a ramp, and then a censoring artefact. Units per listing rises 0.131 → 4.444 from month 1 to month 5, then plateaus at 3.3–4.6 through month 11. Normalised velocity (units per listing per month of age) peaks at 0.889 at 5 months and declines to 0.463 at 10 months — that is the only decay signal, and it reflects older listings being seasonal or already-harvested, not listing fatigue. The 12-month spike (7.845 units/list, $159.32) is not a finding: only 239 listings reach 12 months, from just 6 shops, and they are concentrated in high-performing shops (MartyWallArt, PixelscapeArtStudio). Treat the 10–12 month rows as unreliable.

The zero rate is the real age signal, and it is steep: 96.77% at 1 month, 89.19% at 2, 82.06% at 3, 75.60% at 4, and a floor of 70.58% at 7 months. A listing has roughly a 4-in-5 chance of having made a single sale by month 3 and a 3-in-10 chance of never selling at all. The corpus never shows a listing "going dead" after a good start — but note only 22 shops and 728 listings survive to month 10, so the corpus cannot show what happens to slow starters over 2 years.

Price moves with age in the opposite direction to what you'd expect: mean price is $10.23 at 1 month and $7.04 at 4 months. Newer listings carry more $20+ SKUs (444 of 5,733 at month 1, 7.7%, vs 52 of 2,619 at month 4, 2.0%). Shops launch expensive first, then discount or add cheap volume as they learn.

Method notes and caveats. - Units for §1–7 are Est. Sales Last 6 Mo; units for §8 are Est. Total Sales (lifetime) for both sources, because s17 has no 6-month column. Do not compare §1–7 and §8 revenue figures directly. - Revenue is Price (hidden) × units. It excludes Etsy's ~$0.20 listing fee, transaction fees, advertising spend, and refunds, and assumes the hidden price is the realised price. - All revenue figures are estimates from third-party sales estimators, not Etsy's actual numbers. - Item counts are parsed from titles by regex. The first pass produced false positives (years like 2026, print dimensions like 18x20, listing IDs); the shipped parser requires the number to be adjacent to a plural noun or a "set of N" / "N+" construction, and the output was manually spot-checked across every size bucket. Bundle sizing is reliable; single-vs-set classification is reliable; the exact item count is best-effort. - s17 lane assignment is keyword-based and approximate, validated by per-shop inspection but not ground-truthed. s17 Niche Targeting is 13,035 distinct values over 13,846 rows, so no taxonomy recovery is possible from that field alone. - Listing age is a snapshot field, not a launch date.
