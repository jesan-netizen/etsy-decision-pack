Method & Waves

The brief this pack answers, the wave plan that produced it, and the hard rules it was built under.

Permission granted by the boss: as soon as Wave 1 data lands, dispatch one agent per direction with this exact brief. The boss makes the final decision; agents build the strongest honest case for each direction.

The one rule#

Treat the young winners (56 shops) + 17 master shops as ONE combined corpus: 73 shops, ~40,275 listings. Data: /home/ubuntu/etsy_research/ (Wave 1 outputs), /home/ubuntu/etsy_phase2/ (raw CSVs). No listings created, no Kittl tokens, UK English.

Per-direction deliverable: /home/ubuntu/etsy_research/directions/NN-slug.md (numbered per direction)#

Each direction doc must contain, in this order, with numbers from the corpus at every step:

  1. The direction in one paragraph: lane, aesthetic, audience, the shape strategy (singles/sets/bundles).
  2. The market case from the 73-shop corpus: which shops own this lane, their listings, units, revenue, yield per listing, median prices, price bands that work. Cite the CSV columns and the Wave 1 files.
  3. The competition reality: live Etsy page-one recon for this lane's head terms (from live_recon.md), Everbee vol/comp for its keywords (from everbee_keywords.md + fresh pulls if the agent has them), what a zero-review shop is up against.
  4. First products: the exact first 10 to 20 products (shape, count, subject, price each), designed bundle-backward if bundles are part of the case.
  5. Copy: title formula with 3 example titles, the 13 tag stack for the hero listing, description approach.
  6. Keywords: full keyword table (term, volume, competition, where it goes: title/tag/attributes).
  7. Concepts: the aesthetic bible (palette, medium, light, composition rules), 20+ subject concepts for the first designs, series ideas that scale to 100+ designs without style drift.
  8. Production plan: how the art gets made (AI generation workflow, prompts, upscaling, ratio files), effort per listing, what the mockup set looks like.
  9. Launch simulation, first 30 days: step-by-step, day by day for the first week then weekly: what gets published when, ads or no ads, what metrics to watch, the kill rule.
  10. The strong case FOR (with numbers), the honest case AGAINST (with numbers), what would kill it, and verdict material (no verdict: the boss decides).

Debate (Wave 3)#

After all direction docs: one reconciliation agent reads every doc + the corpus stats, runs the arguments against each other, scores each direction on evidence (market size, yield, competition, production cost, fit with Alfie's doctrine, risk), and produces a ranked table with the trade-offs stated. No final decision: that is the boss's.
