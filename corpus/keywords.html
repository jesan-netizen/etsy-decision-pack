Volume against competition for the six head terms, and the ratio that rules the generic ones out.

Seed terms, volume vs competition (competition = competing listings on Etsy):

keyword monthly volume competition vol/comp
printable wall art 3,435 2,930,832 0.0012

Read: the faith heads have the best volume to competition ratios of the six; the generic heads (printable, vintage) are volume traps with millions of competing listings. Suggestion lists are dominated by misspelling variants with inflated scores (Everbee score artefact), not usable demand signals.

Notable long variants seen in suggestions (real volumes): wall art faithful 958 (comp 8,247), faithfulness wall art 912 (comp 7,007), faithful wall art 905 (comp 7,003), christianity wall art 1,724 (comp 13,253), gallery wall art set 3,633 (comp 137,465), bible verse wall art artful 1,511 (comp 563, artefact variant), wall art bible verses 1,511 (comp 193,828).
