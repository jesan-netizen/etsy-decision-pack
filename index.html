How to read it the brief this pack answers

Etsy Decision Pack: full research base before the boss decides (1 Oct 2026)

Goal: Build the complete evidence base the boss decides from: every shop analysed (56 young winners + 17 master shops = ONE COMBINED CORPUS of 73 shops, ~40,275 listings), patterns, trends, charts, external trend research, route simulations with arguments, and an interactive locally hosted website plus a comprehensive PDF. NO listings, NO Kittl credits, NO decision made by the agent.

Context: - Data on disk: /home/ubuntu/etsy_phase2/ (yw_listings.csv 26,429 rows / 56 shops; shops17_listings.csv 13,847 rows / 17 shops; shop_listing_summary.csv; master_shops_final.csv 76 shops; dead_shop_listings.csv; everbee_starred_shops.csv; comments files; listing_comments/). - Vault notes: Claude OS/0- Etsy/Research (Sep 2026)/ + Etsy Accelerator Transcripts/. - Prior digests: /home/ubuntu/alfie_etsy_digest.md (YouTube), Skool harvests /home/ubuntu/etsy_research/skool_harvest.json + skool_posts.json, Everbee pulls everbee_keywords.md. - Machine small (2 cores, 3GB RAM + 7.9G swap): children use pandas lightly. - Model: children on Space Bunny Alpha (free). Waves of 8.

Deliverables: 1. /home/ubuntu/etsy_research/ working outputs, then EVERYTHING copied into Claude OS/0- Etsy/Decision Pack (Oct 2026)/ on Drive (boss rule: nothing lives only on Ubuntu). 2. Comprehensive PDF "The Research Compendium" (WeasyPrint, charts embedded) on Drive. 3. Locally hosted website (static, charts, all reports) served on a VPS port, link to boss. 4. FULL-BLOWN research project + doc PER DIRECTION (not one-pagers): each direction gets market size from the 73-shop corpus, competition, price evidence, style guide, production plan, launch simulation, risks, argument. Directions include at minimum: oil painterly figurative Christian; scripture text prints; kids wall art; pure vintage bundle shop; Y2K / Swedish Vogue poster style; watercolour minimal; botanical vintage; typography quote prints; plus whatever directions_from_data.md surfaces. 5. Route debate: one agent per direction argues it, a reconciliation agent ranks them. The boss makes the final call.

Waves: - Wave 1 (8 parallel, running): yw corpus analysis; 17-shop analysis; cross-corpus statistics; deep shop case files + directions_from_data.md; vault synthesis; external trends; Alfie transcript deep read; live Etsy page-one recon. - Boss-run in parallel (done): Skool harvest, Everbee keyword pulls. - Wave 2 (8 parallel): full research project per direction, arguing from Wave 1 numbers. - Wave 3: debate/reconciliation ranks directions; website + PDF build; verification; Drive upload + vault-log.

Hard rules: UK English; no listings created; no Kittl tokens spent; nothing ships without the boss reading it; the agent presents evidence and ranked arguments only.

Units basis is mixed and the pack says so everywhere. The 56 Young Winners shops are counted on a real 6-month window (Est. Sales Last 6 Mo). The 17 starred shops only carry Est. Total Sales — lifetime. Totals are therefore valid for ranking lanes against each other and are not a market size. Revenue everywhere is units × listed price: a gross sticker proxy, before Etsy fees, before the standing sale discount, before COGS.
